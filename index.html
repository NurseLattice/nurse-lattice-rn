ABOUT

About RN Quest

RN Quest is a mobile-first study pilot for nursing students and graduates preparing for entry-level registered nurse practice and the NCLEX-RN.

Blueprint

The curriculum follows traditional nursing-school courses and maps each lesson to the eight content areas in the official 2026 NCLEX-RN Test Plan, effective April 1, 2026 through March 31, 2029.

Review the official NCLEX test plan ↗

Use responsibly

Questions and explanations are original educational material. Verify time-sensitive care standards against current institutional policy, official references, and qualified faculty. Never enter patient information.

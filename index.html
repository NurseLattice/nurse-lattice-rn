ABOUT

About NurseLattice RN Quest

NurseLattice RN Quest is a mobile-first study pilot for nursing students and graduates preparing for entry-level registered nurse practice and the NCLEX-RN.

Blueprint

The curriculum follows traditional nursing-school courses and maps each lesson to the eight content areas in the official 2026 NCLEX-RN Test Plan, effective April 1, 2026 through March 31, 2029.

Review the official NCLEX test plan ↗

What this pilot covers

70 lessons and 700 original single-best-answer questions are available. Foundations of Nursing Practice, Health Assessment & Nursing Skills, Pathophysiology, and Pharmacology are complete; later phases in the 184-lesson pathway remain planned. Client Needs labels are author-assigned educational mappings and describe content; they do not make this a blueprint-weighted exam, adaptive test, or full NGN case simulator. Passing a lesson quiz is not evidence of clinical readiness.

Use responsibly

Questions and explanations are original educational material. Verify time-sensitive care standards against current institutional policy, official references, and qualified faculty. Never enter patient information.

Anonymous usage statistics

On the published site, NurseLattice RN Quest records anonymous page visits and learning activity so the project owner can review daily totals. It does not send your account, email, scores, answers, XP, streak, study dates, URLs, or patient information. Learning progress remains only on this device.
